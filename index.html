About Me

I'm Aravind Shiju, an 18-year-old student at Sharjah Indian School / Indian Excellent School with a passion for web development and UI/UX design.

My interest in coding started when I came across creative website designs online. What began as curiosity soon turned into something I love doing—building and improving websites. I enjoy making smooth, user-friendly interfaces and constantly learning new ways to enhance web experiences.

For me, web development is more than just coding; it’s about creating something people can interact with. As I continue learning and growing, I aim to build websites that are both functional and visually appealing.

Learning: Over time, I’ve gained experience in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, building and designing websites with clean and responsive interfaces. Now, I’m expanding my skills by learning React and Node.js, diving deeper into modern web development and creating more dynamic, interactive experiences.
